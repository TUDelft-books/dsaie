`````{tab-set}
````{tab-item} Open question 1: 
Explain how bootstrapping works.

Example answer:
```{toggle} Answer:
Bootstrapping is a frequentist approach to including model uncertainty.
Random subsets of the full dataset are created (with replacement), and a model is trained on each subset.
The mean can then be computed by taking the average of the models, and the variability between the models can be used to estimate the uncertainty.
`````
