# Tree-based Models

Tree-based methods offer a robust alternative to Deep Learning approaches for many case studies. These two recorded lectures will offer you an introduction on the most important topics. The initial lecture is dedicated to decision trees, where we explore their application for both classification and regression tasks. We will delve into how these trees partition data and the principles guiding their growth and pruning decisions. In the second lecture, we turn our attention to more advanced techniques, which are widely employed in supervised machine learning: ensembling and boosting. Here, we analyze the concepts underlying these methods, focusing on random forests and gradient boosted trees. Jupyter Notebooks are included to show you how to implement these techniques with `scikit-learn`, using a real-world dataset for predicting rainfall in Australia.

**Project integration**: Tree-based methods shine with tabular data, but their adaptability allows them to be effectively used for processing time series and even images, given appropriate preprocessing steps.
