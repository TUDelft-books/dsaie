# Clustering and Mixtures

We switch to a completely different class of models: **unsupervised learning**. For these we do not have access to labeled (target) data but instead look for hidden organization patterns in data under the assumption that they come from some lower-dimensional latent representation. In this section we start with models with discrete latent variables, both deterministic and probabilistic.
